Your appointment

Submitting a booking form requests an appointment. Louise will contact you to confirm the arrangements. A request reference is provided when your details have been saved.

Cancellation & arrival

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations may be charged 50% of the session fee.

Please arrive five minutes before your appointment so you can settle comfortably.

The details you share

The appointment form asks for your name, email, phone number, preferred contact method and any optional notes. These details are used to arrange your appointment and communicate with you. Please keep notes brief and avoid including sensitive medical information; discuss that directly with Louise.

Booking details are stored in the appointment system and may be included in appointment notification emails. To ask about access to or deletion of your information, contact Louise using the details below.

This website

The public pages do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The practitioner login uses a session cookie. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to Instagram, Facebook and any book retailer open their respective services, which have their own privacy policies.

The coaching ideas review page can save selections and notes in this browser only when you choose to save them. They are not sent to Louise or submitted to a mailing list. You can clear those saved choices on that page.

Book enquiries

An email link opens your own email app. No message is sent until you send it. Prices, delivery, payment and returns for the book will be provided by the retailer when a sales link becomes available.
