It’s easy to become so used to looking after everyone else that your own needs get quieter. Louise knows that place.

Louise is a qualified nurse with more than three decades of experience in healthcare, including over 20 years as a healthcare leader. Her coaching grew from that experience, family life and her own experience of burnout.

Her starting point is a simple belief: nobody needs to be fixed because nobody is broken. Coaching offers space to explore how you experience life, the thoughts that occupy your attention and possibilities you may not yet be seeing.

It begins with listening. Your sessions are shaped around what matters to you and where you are right now.

CLARITY® Coach · Certified by Jamie Smart, June 2017

A moment to notice

A little space for clarity.

When a snowglobe is shaken, glitter fills the view. As it settles, what was there becomes easier to see.

It’s a simple metaphor for thought. When there is so much moving through our minds, another perspective can be difficult to notice. Coaching offers room to pause and explore, without expecting you to have all the answers.
