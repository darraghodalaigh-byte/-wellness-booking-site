The thought at the heart of it

Perhaps the okayness
you’ve been searching for
was never actually missing.

It was just temporarily hidden.

There was a time Louise genuinely believed she was broken. Not struggling. Not having a hard patch. Broken.

Deeply OK grew from seeing life differently. These 99 short insight posts offer warmth, humour and radical honesty, pointing towards the possibility that we are not as far from ourselves as we think.

Publication planned for 10 October 2026, World Mental Health Day.
