# Soul to Sole by Louise — redesign brief

Build a polished, personal, editorial website for Louise O’Dalaigh that makes three distinct paths immediately clear: reflexology and treatments, transformation through coaching, and the book Deeply OK. Preserve her real biography, portrait, brand character, existing treatment prices in euros, testimonials and working booking/admin capabilities.

Visual thesis: space to return to yourself. Warm paper, deep forest green and the existing plum identity, oversized literary serif type, restrained gold, quietly cinematic water imagery. The book has its own sky-blue/navy chapter. Use composition and typography rather than repetitive cards. Keep copy personal and specific.

Create dedicated home, reflexology, coaching, book, about and appointment pages. Provide four visibly proposed coaching directions on a separate noindex review page so Louise can compare and combine ideas without inventing packages or promises. Selections and notes stay on the visitor’s device and can be downloaded.

Desktop: generous layouts, gentle hover lift and image brightness, subtle pointer response, soft transitions. Mobile: native scrolling, readable type, generous touch targets, no hover dependencies, no scroll trapping. Respect reduced-motion preferences and support keyboard navigation, visible focus, robust loading/error states and useful content before JavaScript.

Book: use the supplied cover and real description, 99 reflections, 10 October 2026 launch, paperback/hardback/ebook format selection. Never invent prices, stock, previews or transactions. Enable genuine retailer/waitlist destinations only when configured; otherwise offer a clearly labelled book enquiry. Retain the underlying ability to add real sale URLs.

Quality gate: review desktop/mobile screenshots; check every route, menu, format selector, coaching review controls and contact link; exercise booking success, validation, unavailable times and errors in local test data; protect the admin from untrusted booking text; verify the deployed version at the public hosting URL. Custom domain connection is separate from domain purchase and depends on hosting/DNS access.
